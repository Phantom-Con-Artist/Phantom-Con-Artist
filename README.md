## 🕒 3 Days Since the Migration

It's been 3 days since the global migration from **Orbis** to **The Saga**, and honestly, it was rough. Rougher than I expected. But it's done, and I wanted to take a moment to actually talk about how we got here, not just the changelog version, the real one.

**A little history.** I've been working on this idea for more than a year now. For a long stretch of that time, I was writing it in **Notepad**. No syntax highlighting, no autocomplete, nothing. I built v1 that way. I'm not saying it to be funny, it's genuinely just what I had. I work off a mid-tier laptop, nothing else, and even that laptop has been fighting me the whole time, weighed down by a bunch of leftover processes from earlier experiments I never fully cleaned up. Switching to VS Code recently felt like getting a proper set of tools for the first time, and it made a real difference in how I work. Along with that, I've also been leaning on AI tools, for debugging, for rapid drafting, for getting unstuck faster. I've also relied on it a lot for documentation specifically, since English isn't my native language, and getting my ideas across clearly matters a lot to me. I'm not going to pretend otherwise or apologize for any of it. Building an engine like this from scratch, solo, is not something one person can realistically do alone anymore, especially now that v2 has grown so much bigger in scope than v1 ever was. Honestly, it's given me a huge boost in what I can get done, and I'd rather be upfront about that than let anyone assume this was built in some purist, unassisted way it wasn't.

Right now, all of that effort is going into **Sorophy™ v2**.

There's good news and bad news, and I want to be honest about both.

**The good news:** Sorophy™ v2, Krono, has entered its testing phase. I'll be sharing a direct comparison against v1 and walking through everything I've completed, so it's not just me telling you it's better, you'll be able to see it. It'll be out once I've tested it properly across different platforms. The jump from v1 to v2 is a much bigger architectural leap than I anticipated, and I've hit real limits I have to work around. I'm putting everything I have into getting it right rather than getting it out fast.

**The harder news:** I've had to cut some features from this release and push them to after v2. That wasn't an easy call, but I'd rather ship something solid and honest about its scope than something rushed. The full vision, a complete **Temporal Graph Evolution Runtime**, is still exactly what I'm building toward. Nothing promised has been abandoned, just resequenced.

And since I want this update to be genuine, I'll say the part that's harder to write. For the past three-plus months, this project has been nearly all I've done. I've been working 40+ hour weeks on top of everything else in my life, and it's taken a toll. I've lost weight, and I've been dealing with bad migraines. I'm not saying that for sympathy, just because I think it's honest to admit that this has cost me something real, not just time. I'm choosing to see it through to a stable v2 before my university commitments pull my attention elsewhere, and after that, I'm planning to actually take a real break before slowly starting the desktop application. I know I need to.

I know how this might look, someone who joined a week ago, with no followers and no community yet, pouring this much of himself into it. I don't have a tidy answer for why except that this project means something to me. It's the thing I actually want to be building. And I didn't want to step away for a while and leave it in a half-finished state, even if right now the only person who'd notice is whoever happens to be reading this.

So — thank you, genuinely, for reading this far. **Sorophy™ v2 will be out of beta soon**, and I'm doing my best to make it worth the wait.

## 🛑 Orbpad Development Has Ended

There is one important project update I also want to make clear.

**I am stopping active maintenance of Orbpad.**

The **Orbpad repository will remain public**, and all existing releases will remain available for download.

Orbpad v1.0.0 is a standalone application and does **not** use the Sorophy™ backend.

Orbpad v1.0.1, however, runs on the **Orb Engine v1.0.0-beta.2** backend — making it part of the early history of what eventually became Sorophy™.

You can still explore the project and its releases here:

<p align="center">

<a href="https://github.com/Phantom-Con-Artist/Orbpad">
  📝 <b>Visit Orbpad</b>
</a>

</p>

### Why am I stopping Orbpad?

Orbpad gradually evolved into something much larger than I originally intended.

It was slowly becoming:

> **Everything + Orb Engine support**

And while that was an interesting direction, it moved Orbpad away from its original role within the **Myriad Ecosystem**.

Continuing to redesign and maintain Orbpad around an ever-expanding set of features and Orb Engine support would require a substantial amount of time while pulling focus away from the development of **Sorophy™**, **The Saga**, and the wider Myriad Ecosystem.

Rather than continuing to turn Orbpad into something it was never meant to be, I have decided to stop active development and preserve it as it is.

The **Orbpad repository will remain public**, and its existing releases will remain available for anyone who wants to download and use them.

In fact, **Orbpad v1.0.0 is an important part of the project's history**. It is a standalone application and an early demonstration of the potential that eventually led to Orb Engine and, ultimately, Sorophy™.

Orbpad v1.0.1 later became one of the earliest applications built around the Orb Engine v1 lineage.

So while Orbpad's development ends here, the project itself remains available as a reminder of where everything started.

More or less, **Orbpad will remain a remembrance of how The Saga began.**

### 🧡 Orbpad Was the Beginning

Orbpad v1.0.0 is more than just an old application.

It is one of the earliest demonstrations of what the **Orb Engine — now Sorophy™ — was capable of becoming.**

It was the software that helped prove the idea could actually work.

So even though Orbpad is no longer being maintained, it will remain public as a piece of the project's history.

In a way, **Orbpad is a remembrance of how everything started.**
